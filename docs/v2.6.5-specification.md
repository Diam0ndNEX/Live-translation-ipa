# ScreenLingo 2.6.5 (build 130): lower and steadier delay, as built

Base: `claude/screenlingo-v2.6.4`. Work branch: `claude/screenlingo-v2.6.5`. Draft PR into the base branch.
Built from the 2.6.3 device report (build 128, 502 captions, Legacy, pt>en). **Nothing was compiled or run in chat**; CI compiles and runs the Swift tests. **No speed gain is claimed**: it is unverified until a report with Diagnostics data (same content, Legacy) shows it.

## 1. What the report showed (numbers from its tables)
- Delay from first sighting to the shown caption: P50 511, P90 950, P99 1659 ms. Of that, first sighting to confirmation is P50 181 ms and confirmation to shown is P50 323 ms.
- 330 of 502 captions were translated only after confirmation (P50 363 ms from confirmation to shown). 119 came from memory (3 ms). Only 53 had an early translation that was running or ready.
- 326 captions had no early call at all. Skip reasons for first readings (counts of readings, one caption has several): low confidence 320, adaptive low yield 128, model busy 167, same text as before 292, behind the shown caption 256. The early calls that did run started a median 174 ms after the first reading, about when confirmation happens.
- Slow tail: the 8 slowest captions were slow inside the Apple call (1.0 to 2.7 s). Two calls never returned and were abandoned at 6 s.

## 2. Learned early start (switch `learnedEarlyStart`, default ON, report arm `learnedEarlyStartAB`)
Before: a first reading started its early translation only when every judged line had OCR confidence of at least the fixed setting (0.85).
Now (`Shared/TranslationSystem+V265.swift`, `App/Engine/TranslatorEngine+EarlyStartLearning.swift`):
- Every first reading of a caption is noted with its lowest line confidence and its normalized text (all first readings, also refused ones, so the sample is not biased by the limit).
- When the caption is confirmed, the noted text is compared with the confirmed text (`ConfirmedSourceIdentity.normalize`). A caption that never confirms counts as not equal once it falls out of the 12-caption book.
- `SpecConfidenceLearner` keeps the last 160 comparisons. After 24 comparisons the limit is the lowest confidence (0.55 to 0.95, steps of 0.05) at or above which at least 90% of at least 8 first readings were already the final text. If no limit qualifies, the fixed setting stays.
- Used at the two places that admit an early start (first reading, and behind a running caption). Cool mode keeps its fixed 0.95. All other rules are unchanged (growing subtitles, armed deferral, the adaptive window, busy lane, same text).
- Nothing depends on a language, a video, a speaker label or a screen position.
Cost when the learned limit is lower: a wrong first reading wastes one quiet call. The adaptive window still backs off when early calls are rarely reused.

## 3. Sooner hang limit (switch `quickHangLimit`, default ON, report arm `quickHangLimitAB`)
`ModelStallLimit`: floor 3.5 s (was 6 s) and 4 times the slow end of the last 50 calls (was 6 times). The longest call that returned in the report took 2.7 s. A call that returns after being given up on still widens the limit (2.6.0). Cold limit (before 5 calls) stays 15 s.

`Tests/HotPathAB`: switches default on and turn off alone; the learner (nothing learned, too few samples, all-final lowers to the floor, wrong readings keep the limit above them, nothing qualifies keeps the fixed limit); the first-reading book (first only, taken once, eviction reported); the sooner and the 2.6.0 hang limit. The 2.6.0 limit test now pins the sooner limit off. `scripts/test_three_modes.py`: 2.6.5 contracts.
